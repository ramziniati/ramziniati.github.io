// 01 — About Me

WHO AM I

I'm an Electronics & Communication Engineering student at the École Nationale Polytechnique d'Alger, the top-ranked engineering school in Algeria. I'm building a strong foundation in both theory and practice of modern AI systems.

My interests span the full hardware-to-software stack, from Embedded Systems and Digital Design to FPGA Engineering, Signal Processing, RF & Telecommunications Engineering, Network and Security too, and Computer Architecture, all grounded in a strong passion for Mathematics and Physics. I'm genuinely curious about how computers are built from the ground up, how signals travel and connects our world-wide, and how hardware and software meet.

What genuinely draws me in is Generative AI and Natural Language Processing not just as tools, but as systems worth understanding from the ground up. I learn fast, think analytically, and enjoy digging into problems until I truly understand what's going on.

Beyond the technical, I'm an author: I published my first book on Classical Mechanics which became a reference used by over 83% of ENP students and foreign students alike.
